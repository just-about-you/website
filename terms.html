What will be here

What the app is offered as, what it is not, and what happens to your data if the project stops. That last one matters more than usual here, and the answer is already true: your records are on your phone, so they outlive us.

What is already binding

The privacy account describes what the app actually does, and is held to the code by the tests on the checking page. Those do not wait for this page.

This is not medical care

Your Health records what you tell it and shows it back to you. It does not interpret readings, and nothing in it is a substitute for talking to a doctor.
