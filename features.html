Features

What it does, a page at a time.

Each of these has its own page rather than a paragraph, because a list of eighteen headings is a thing people scroll past. Every screenshot is the real app.

Day to day

Recording a reading

A tile on the home screen for each number you track, and the two taps that put a value in it.

Medications

Grouped by the hour you take them, counted by the dose rather than the medication.

Tasks

Appointments, repeat prescriptions, forms. The health admin that has no number attached.

Backup and restore

One encrypted file holding everything, with a password only you have.

Friends and carers

What a friend sees, what a carer sees, and why both start switched off.

Making it yours

Eighteen palettes with three of them free, a lock on the app, the widget, and what you track at all.

What holds all of it together

Your records are encrypted on your own phone. Nothing about you reaches anyone until you switch that part on yourself. By default a caregiver then sees only that you logged something, not the number — with two exceptions you control: a single reading you approve when they ask for it, and any medication you choose to tag for them.
