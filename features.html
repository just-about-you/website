Features

What it does, a page at a time.

Each of these has its own page rather than a paragraph, because a list of eighteen headings is a thing people scroll past. Every screenshot is the real app.

Day to day

Recording a reading

The circle on the home screen, and the two taps that put a number in it.

Trends

Every number you track across a stretch of days — including the ones you have never recorded.

Medications

Grouped by the hour you take them, counted by the dose rather than the medication.

Tasks

Appointments, repeat prescriptions, forms. The health admin that has no number attached.

Backup and restore

One encrypted file holding everything, with a password only you have.

App Lock

A second lock on the app itself, for when the phone leaves your hand.

Appearance

Light, dark, or whatever your phone says — and a colour family that runs through every screen.

Pages still being written

These all ship in the app today. Their pages are waiting on screenshots that show them working rather than sitting empty, which is worth the wait — a picture of a disabled button teaches the wrong thing.

Checking in

One tap a day telling the people you chose that you are all right, and a short message if something needs saying.

Friends and the care circle

No usernames, no directory, no search. You hand out a single-use invite or nobody reaches you.

Giving a caregiver access

They see that you recorded something, not what it was — and only for the categories you switch on.

The home-screen widget

One number per page, on your home screen, without opening the app.

What holds all of it together

Your records are encrypted on your own phone. Nothing about you reaches anyone until you switch that part on yourself, and even then a caregiver sees that you logged something rather than what it was.
