About

Why this exists.

Most health apps ask for an account before they will hold a single number, and then keep those numbers on a server for as long as the company lasts. This one was built the other way round: the readings never leave the phone, and the parts that need a server were designed to carry as little as they possibly could.

The idea

Someone tracking their blood pressure usually has a reason, and often has someone who worries about them. Those two things pull in opposite directions: the person who worries wants to know you are all right, and you may not want to hand over a stream of numbers about your body.

The compromise the app makes is a yes-or-no. A carer sees that you logged today, which answers the question they actually have. If they want a number, they ask, and you decide each time.

How we talk about privacy

Claims about privacy are easy to make and hard to check, so the privacy page is written to be checked. It lists what is stored, what leaves the phone, what a stolen database would contain, and — deliberately — the three places where the strong version of the claim stops being true.

We would rather you heard those from us than found them yourself.

This is not medical care

Your Health is a self-help tool for recording how you feel and tracking things over time. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any illness, and it is not a substitute for professional advice. If a result concerns you, contact a doctor.
