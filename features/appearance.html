Appearance

Fifteen themes, whole ones.

Comfort matters more than decoration here. A darker screen at night, or one with more contrast, can be the difference between reading something easily and giving up on it.

All features

What you can change

A theme. Each of the fifteen is one complete set of colours that runs through every screen, rather than an accent on one bar.

Some are bright and some are dark, and each row says which. The theme is the choice: there is no separate light and dark switch, and no follow-your-phone option. Text size is its own row, directly underneath, and is worth a look if anything feels small.

One list, one decision

Fifteen rows, each with its colours shown beside it and the word Bright or Dark. Tapping one changes the whole app at once.

Why it is more than a preference

Two ends of this app’s audience are a long way apart, and one of them is reading a phone at arm’s length in a bright room.

Making the whole app change together, rather than tinting one bar, is what makes that actually help. Every one of the fifteen is held to the same contrast floor, so picking the one you like is not a trade against being able to read it.

Finding it

Open the menu, then Settings

Appearance is the second row, after Your name, with Text size directly under it.

Everything here runs on your own phone

No account, no email address, and no reading of yours in our database. You can check that rather than take our word for it.
