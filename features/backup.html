Backup

One file, one password, no way back.

Your records live on your phone, which is the point and also the risk. A backup is the answer, and it has a sharp edge worth knowing about first.

All features

What a backup holds

Your readings, your medications, your tasks and your settings, in one file, locked with a password you choose. You pick where it goes and we never see it.

Restoring it puts everything back where it was, on this phone or a new one.

Back up, or bring one back

Both live together under Privacy and your data.

Why the password cannot be recovered

There is no account, so there is nobody holding a copy of anything to reset. That is what makes the rest of this app true, and it is also why a lost password means the file cannot be opened by anyone.

Not by you, and not by us. It is the one place where the design costs you something, so we would rather say it plainly than bury it.

Choosing a password

Let the app make one up

It writes a long password for you, which is safer than one you invent. Write it down somewhere safe before you go on.

Keep the hint vague

Whatever you type is stored as plain text inside the file, so anyone holding the file can read the hint. Make it remind you, not describe it.

If you lost the phone, not the file

A backup file is one answer. Recovery is the other, and it is for getting your friends and carers back rather than your readings.

A six-digit code, to a phone you still have

The code goes to the devices already set up under your account. It is single use, it lasts ten minutes, and five wrong tries destroy it and start again.

What recovery brings back, and what it does not

Your friends, the people you care for and the arrangements between you. Not your readings: those live on the phone, and a backup file is the only thing that carries them.

Everything here runs on your own phone

No account, no email address, and no reading of yours in our database. You can check that rather than take our word for it.
