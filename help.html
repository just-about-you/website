Help & support

How things work.

The app keeps its own explanations short. This is the longer version, including the parts that are genuinely unrecoverable — which you want to know about before you need to, not after.

Getting started

There is nothing to sign up for.

Open the app and it works. The first run asks a few questions to set up the home screen, and you can change every answer later in Settings.

Readings are entered from the wheel on the home screen — one wedge per thing you track. Anything that is not a number you record and move on from (a symptom journal, a cycle calendar, a weight trend) gets its own page instead.

People

Invites, and what a carer can see.

You send an invite, and you name it

To connect with someone you create a single-use invite and send it to them however you normally talk — a message, an email, in person. When you make it you say who it is for, so that name is already waiting when they accept. There is no username to search and no directory to appear in.

An invite works once and can be withdrawn before it is used. Two people redeeming the same one is not possible; the first use spends it.

Names live on your phone, not ours

You label the people you connect with, and those labels stay on your device. We never receive them. That means the other person sees whatever name they saved for you, on their own phone — and there is no name of yours anywhere on our side to leak.

Check who sent an incoming request

Because we hold no names, the app genuinely cannot tell you who an incoming request is from — only that somebody used your invite. It says so plainly, above the accept and decline buttons. If you were not expecting it, check with the person directly before accepting.

What a carer can see

For each category you switch on, they see whether you logged it today — a tick or a blank. They do not see the reading. If they want a number they have to ask, and each request is approved or refused on its own: saying yes once does not say yes to the next one.

Switching a category off removes the days it already recorded, not just future ones. Sharing is off until you turn it on.

A day with no record is not a missed day. A day only has a record once a phone has sent one, so a gap means "no record" — someone who installed the app last week is not shown as having ignored it for a month.

Backups

The part that cannot be undone.

Because your readings are only on your phone, a backup file is the one copy that can exist anywhere else. You choose whether to make one, and where it goes.

Read this before you need it

A backup is encrypted with a passphrase you choose. If you lose that passphrase, the file cannot be opened — not by you, and not by us. There is no reset link, because there is no account behind it and nothing on our side to reset.

The app offers to generate a passphrase for you, and that is the option to take. It is far stronger than one you invent, and you only need to write it down once.

The hint you set is written into the file in plain text. Anybody holding the file can read the hint, so do not make it the passphrase.

If you lose the phone and have no backup, the readings are gone. That is the direct cost of them not being on our servers, and it is a real trade rather than a detail.

On the device

App Lock and the widget.

What App Lock is for

It puts the app behind your device biometrics or a PIN you set for it, which protects against somebody picking up your unlocked phone. It is not the thing encrypting your data — that happens whether App Lock is on or off.

The widget has an arrow, not a swipe

It shows one metric at a time and turns over on its own; the arrow moves to the next. Android does not deliver taps on individual items in a scrolling widget, so a page with both a chart and a working button cannot be one — the arrow is the way to move.

Deleting everything

"Delete all data" empties the database, clears your settings, cancels scheduled reminders, and overwrites the freed space so the old rows are not left readable in the file. It does not remove a backup you already made — that file is yours.

When something is wrong

The three we get asked about most.

Notifications are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in Android settings, and that battery optimisation is not restricting it. Some phones are aggressive about background apps by default, and Samsung devices especially so.

An invite will not work

Invites are single-use — if it has already been accepted, it is spent, and a fresh one is needed. Check it has not been withdrawn, and that it has not expired.

Trends looks empty

Trends lists every metric you track whether or not it has data yet. Charts fill in as you log. Nothing is hidden for being empty — that is deliberate, so an entry point never disappears on you.

Still stuck?

There is a "Help & contact" row in the app's settings that reaches us. If something in the privacy account looks wrong, we would particularly like to hear about that one.
