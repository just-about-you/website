Help & support

How things work.

The app keeps its own explanations short. This is the longer version, including the parts that are genuinely unrecoverable — which you should know about before you need to.

Getting started

There is nothing to sign up for. Open the app and it works. The first run asks a few questions to set up the home screen, and you can change every answer later in Settings.

Readings are entered from the wheel on the home screen — one wedge per thing you track. Anything that is not a number you record and move on from (a symptom journal, a cycle calendar, a weight trend) gets its own card instead.

Friends, and people who care for you

You add someone by sharing a short code. There are no usernames to search and no directory to appear in — if you have not given someone your code, they cannot find you.

The name you pick is yours alone

The name you choose stays on your phone. It is not sent to us, and the people you connect with see whatever name they saved for you on their own device. That is why the app suggests not using your real name: it serves no purpose outside your own screen.

What a carer can see

For each category you switch on, they see whether you logged it today — a tick or a blank. They do not see the reading. If they want a number they have to ask, and each request is approved or refused on its own: saying yes once does not say yes to the next one.

Switching a category off removes the days it already recorded, not just future ones. Sharing is off until you turn it on.

A day with no record is not a missed day. Rows only exist when a phone has synced, so a gap means "no record" — someone who installed the app last week is not shown as having ignored it for a month.

Backups, and what cannot be recovered

Because your readings are only on your phone, a backup file is the one copy that can exist anywhere else. You choose whether to make one and where it goes.

Read this part before you need it

A backup is encrypted with a passphrase you choose. If you lose that passphrase, the file cannot be opened — not by you and not by us. There is no reset link, because there is no account behind it and nothing on our side to reset.

The hint you set is written into the file in plain text. Anyone holding the file can read the hint, so do not make it the passphrase.

If you lose the phone and have no backup, the readings are gone. That is the direct cost of them never being on our servers, and it is a real trade rather than a detail.

App Lock

App Lock puts the app behind your device biometrics or a PIN you set for it. It protects against someone picking up your unlocked phone. It is not the thing encrypting your data — that happens regardless of whether App Lock is on.

The home-screen widget

The widget shows one metric at a time and rotates through them on its own. The arrow moves to the next one. It shows a small bar chart and the latest value, and tapping through opens the app at that metric.

There is no swipe. Android's widget system does not deliver taps on individual items in a scrolling widget, so a page with both a chart and a working button cannot be one — the arrow is the way to move.

When something is wrong

Notifications are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in Android settings, and that battery optimisation is not restricting it. Some phones are aggressive about background apps by default.

A friend's code will not add

Codes are checked exactly. Confirm the characters, and make sure they have opened the People screen at least once — a code has to be registered before anyone can add it.

Trends looks empty

Trends lists every metric you track, whether or not it has data yet. Charts fill in as you log. Nothing is hidden because it is empty.

Still stuck?

There is a "Help & contact" row in the app's settings that will reach us. If something in the privacy account looks wrong, we would particularly like to know.
